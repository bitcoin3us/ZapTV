Channel 01 · Zaps

ZapTV!

Your zaps, live on your desk. Scan your npub, connect a wallet, and every zap that lands announces itself with a full-screen lightning strike, while a receive QR invites the room to send the next one.

  • Nostr-native. Scan your npub with the built-in camera; your profile picture, name, and npub.cash Lightning address appear automatically.
  • Works with your wallet. LNbits, Nostr Wallet Connect, or a watch-only on-chain xpub via any Blockbook instance, including self-hosted.
  • Made to be watched. New zaps strike like lightning, then blink for 30 seconds. Show 1 to 21 recent transactions, sorted by newest or largest.
  • Comments, with the ⚡ intact. Zap comments render with full emoji support.
  • Safe in public. Display-only: no keys, no balance on screen, xpubs redacted from logs.

A first: ZapTV is the first Nostr-native app on MicroPythonOS, built around zaps, not balances.

Try it · Live

Tune in with your npub

Paste your npub and this little TV fetches your real Nostr profile and your latest zaps from public relays, right here in the browser. Nothing is stored, nothing leaves your machine except the relay queries.

Channel 02 · The chain

BlockTV

The whole network, always on air. Compose your own pages from sixteen data fields: block height, price, moscow time, fees, halving countdown, charts, even your latest zap. Then let the numbers roll in odometer-style.

  • It never lies to you. Every chart point is a real market sample. Stale data shades orange, then red. Outages are back-filled with what the price actually did, never drawn as a flat line.
  • Sixteen fields, your layout. One huge number or up to eight tiles per page, unlimited pages, drag to reorder.
  • Charts that fit a microcontroller. 24 h to a full four-year halving cycle, one plotted point per pixel column, all sharing a single streaming fetch.
  • Bitcoin-native extras. Latest zap on screen via your npub; wallet balance over Nostr Wallet Connect. Watch-only, always.
  • Built like an appliance. Instant restarts with last-known values, your own colours, optional new-block flash, full keypad control.
  • Private if you want. Point it at your own self-hosted mempool instance.

The platform

Runs on MicroPythonOS

MicroPythonOS is an open-source operating system for microcontrollers: an app framework, launcher, app store, and over-the-air updates: the smartphone experience, on hardware that costs about as much as a pizza.

What you need

Board
ESP32-S3 + 320×240 display
e.g. Waveshare ESP32-S3 Touch LCD 2
Touch
optional; both apps run keypad-only
Network
WiFi, 2.4 GHz
Cable
USB-C, for the one-time install
Cost
roughly $15 to $25 all-in

How to run it

  1. 1
    Flash MicroPythonOS from your browser.

    Plug the board in over USB and use the web installer at install.micropythonos.com. No toolchain, no terminal.

  2. 2
    Join your WiFi.

    Pick your network on the device the first time it boots.

  3. 3
    Install the apps.

    Open the on-device app store and install ZapTV and BlockTV. Updates arrive over the air.
